# Early-access safety operations

Operational owner: Pebble development team. Public support/privacy/child-safety
contact: `pebblesupport.help@gmail.com`, confirmed monitored by the operator for
this release. An implemented report receipt is not a guarantee of moderation.

## Required ongoing human work

- Monitor the support mailbox and the restricted `safety_private.reports` queue.
  In-app reports are stored in that queue; they are **not automatically emailed**.
  Assign someone on the team to check it at least daily and whenever an urgent
  concern is received. This is an operational responsibility, not an automated SLA.
- Review receipts and necessary context only with authorized administrative access.
  Do not put private reports, messages, tokens or photos into public issues/logs.
- Prioritize threats, exploitation, suspected minors and account compromise. For
  immediate danger, contact appropriate emergency services. Do not ask a reporter
  to download or resend suspected CSAM, and do not circulate that material.
- For suspected CSAM/exploitation: restrict access, preserve necessary evidence
  under a lawful, access-controlled process, consult applicable reporting duties,
  and report to the appropriate authority (including NCMEC where applicable).
  The public child-safety contact must remain reachable. Obtain qualified advice
  on reporting/retention obligations; app code is not a substitute for that process.
- Restrict offending accounts or remove content through a controlled administrator
  session. Verify the action and its cross-feature consequences; blocking is not
  an administrative ban and does not erase existing shared history.
- Keep a restricted incident record of receipt, reviewer, decision, timestamps and
  appeal/correction handling. Do not promise a resolution time that cannot be met.

## Deletion requests

Public resource: `https://pebble.garden/delete-account`. The app also has a real
Settings deletion path. Verify ownership before acting on email requests; never
request passwords or sign-in codes. Explain retained conversation/safety/log data
and avoid claiming that uninstalling or signing out deletes server data. Handle
manual requests in a restricted session and record confirmation, not simulated
success. No universal retention duration or email-completion deadline is configured.

## Release boundaries

The app is 18+ based on declared birthdate plus server admission checks, not identity
verification. Saved birthday corrections go through support. Safeguards reduce
risk but cannot establish that every account's age is genuine. Continue abuse
monitoring after launch. Before a later Play submission, identify the responsible
child-safety contact and complete required declarations; publication is separate.
